Project Purpose & Scope

This innovation addresses water pollution, greenhouse gas emissions, and high energy costs in conventional biological wastewater treatment. By encapsulating microalgae (Chlorella vulgaris) with microscopic gas bubbles via a specialized coaxial nozzle, the resulting beads achieve spontaneous floating buoyancy on the water surface.

4-Step Technological Mechanism:

  • Coaxial Nozzle Micro-encapsulation: Gas bubble injection into alginate polymer matrix.
  • Spontaneous Buoyancy: 100% sunlight exposure without mechanical mixing or aeration.
  • Rapid 45-Hour Phycoremediation: High-efficiency nitrogen and phosphorus uptake.
  • Zero-Energy Harvesting: Effortless surface skimming yielding protein-enriched aquaculture feed (+200% protein).
